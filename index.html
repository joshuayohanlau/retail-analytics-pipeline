Data Quality Findings
Source Issue Business Impact Severity

products Negative unit prices (~4 rows) Breaks downstream revenue calculations High
products Inconsistent category labels Fragments category-level reporting Medium

orders Duplicate order IDs Double-counts orders in revenue reporting High
payments ~90 amount mismatches vs order totals Revenue reconciliation failures High
inventory Mixed date formats (~15% of rows) Date parsing errors across the date spine Medium
web_activity ~2% bot traffic in session data Skews conversion rate and session metrics Low
